OpenRA2 and OpenYR are community-led, open-source reconstructions of
*Command & Conquer: Red Alert 2* and *Command & Conquer: Yuri's Revenge*
respectively: the original engines rebuilt standalone as a single,
conventional C++ project, playable today.

OpenRA2, OpenYR and OpenRA2YR are independent community projects and are not
affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts.
